# Copywriting and CTA Specification

| Element | Recommended copy | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eyebrow | FREE 7-PAGE POKOPIA PRINTABLE | Verified product format/page count |
| Headline | START YOUR POKOPIA ADVENTURE ORGANIZED. | Approved direction; supported by product purpose |
| Supporting copy | Plan your next Pokopia session with a simple printable guide for goals, daily priorities, island ideas, and rare finds. | Supported by offer benefits and preview pages |
| CTA | GET MY FREE QUICK START | Proposed label; retains current claim behavior |
| Form reassurance | Free to claim. No card or password required. | Current flow is free; recovery is passwordless |
| Preview heading | SEE WHAT YOU’LL USE BEFORE YOU CLAIM IT. | Proposed framing |
| How access works | Enter your email. Claim the guide. Choose US Letter or A4 when your access is available. | Supported; does not promise delivery timing |
| Final CTA | START YOUR NEXT SESSION WITH A SIMPLE PLAN. | Proposed reminder |
| Existing access | Already have access? Return to your Quick Start Pack. | Proposed label; preserves recovery route |
| Fan-made notice | Fan-made by Sugarfield Studio. Not affiliated with Nintendo or The Pokémon Company. | Existing footer meaning preserved |

## Alternative hero for later testing

**YOUR NEXT POKOPIA SESSION, WITH A SIMPLE PLAN.**

Use the same supporting copy, CTA, and proof pages. This is a test hypothesis, not a replacement approved for implementation.

## Copy constraints

- Do not claim immediate delivery, customer results, scarcity, endorsements, ratings, or official affiliation.
- Do not describe unavailable sixth/seventh preview pages.
- Do not turn the final CTA into another submission mechanism.
- Keep privacy wording separate from advertising-consent disclosures, which are unchanged.

## Approval requirement

Approve the recommended copy before it replaces any customer-facing strings.
