# Landing Page Section Blueprint

## Proposed sequence

1. **Hero:** Free badge, benefit-led headline, concise description, three fact chips, one email form, low-friction reassurance, and a compact composition of three real pages.
2. **Player problems:** Three short cards: choose a focus, remember small tasks, save ideas.
3. **What’s inside:** A five-page approved-artwork gallery with page names and concise supported uses.
4. **How access works:** Enter email → claim the guide → access Letter/A4 PDFs when access is available. No email-timing promise.
5. **Why useful:** Supported outcomes tied to the actual pages: session focus, daily priorities, sketching, and notes.
6. **Final CTA:** Same message and CTA; implementation scrolls/focuses the existing hero form, preserving one submission implementation and analytics event sequence.
7. **Compact footer:** Existing fan-made disclaimer and privacy/terms links.

## Proposed interaction rules

- One visual primary CTA per section; no paid-offer promotion on this free landing page.
- Existing access remains reachable by the header anchor and secondary recovery content.
- The final CTA must not introduce a second form endpoint, duplicate form analytics, or independent client state.
- The fixed non-modal consent panel remains unchanged and overlays according to the current implementation.

## Assumptions requiring approval

The proposed shorter hero and placement of recovery below initial claim content are design decisions, not evidence of a known conversion lift.
